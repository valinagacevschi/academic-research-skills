## Answer summary

1. **Three tiers.** Romanian respiratory authors realistically target three tiers:
   - (a) international society/flagship respiratory journals: ERJ, ERJ Open Research, AJRCCM, CHEST, Thorax, Respiratory Medicine, IJTLD (and its OA siblings);
   - (b) MDPI/OA "general medicine" titles, e.g. *Medicina*. These dominate Romanian output. About 34% of Romanian-affiliated articles were in MDPI in 2021, and about 40% of roughly 20,000 Romanian ISI articles in 2025, per the UBB Scientific Council report;
   - (c) domestic journals: *Pneumologia*, RJME, *Maedica*, *Farmacia*, *Romanian Journal of Internal Medicine*, and others.
2. ***Pneumologia* has weak indexing.** It is the Romanian Society of Pneumology (SRP) journal: English-only, free to publish, open access (Sciendo/Paradigm). It is in **Scopus** (CiteScore 0.2). It is **not in WoS Core**: it is absent from both UEFISCDI lists. It was **deselected from MEDLINE**, which covered it only from 1999 to 2016. For career metrics it is a low-weight venue. Its practical value is that it is the publication path the SRP congress points abstracts toward.
3. **Abstract formats differ a lot**, and ARS must model each one explicitly:

   | Venue | Abstract format |
   |---|---|
   | ERJ / ERJ OR | 250 words, four paragraphs, no headings (question / methods / results / answer) |
   | AJRCCM | 250 words with headings: Rationale, Objectives, Methods, Measurements and Main Results, Conclusions |
   | CHEST | Background, Research Question, Study Design and Methods, Results, Interpretation |
   | Thorax | Structured, ≤250 words, plus "key messages" |
   | IJTLD | 200 words: Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion |
   | *Medicina* (MDPI) | About 300 words, one paragraph with headings |
   | *Farmacia* | ≤1500 **characters**, in **Romanian and English** |
   | ERS Congress | ≤1810 **characters** including spaces (tables and figures consume characters); title capped separately at 250 characters |
   | SRP Congress | ≤300 words **in each of Romanian and English**, in a single document, no tables or figures |

4. **A Romanian abstract is required only by *Farmacia* and the SRP congress.** No international journal requires one. *Pneumologia* is English-only. IJTLD translates abstracts into French itself.
5. **AI-use disclosure is universal, but the placement differs by venue:**
   - ERS (March 2026): declared at submission for all uses, *including grammar and translation*. In the manuscript, the declaration goes in Acknowledgements (drafting), Methods (analysis or code) or the figure legend (diagrams). AI-generated experimental photos are not allowed. Patient data must not go into non-enterprise AI tools.
   - Elsevier (CHEST, Respiratory Medicine): a dedicated section before the references. No AI-generated or AI-altered images.
   - BMJ (Thorax): contributor section, plus Methods if AI was used in the research.
   - ATS/OUP (AJRCCM): Methods or Acknowledgments.
   - IJTLD: declare the *proportion* of the manuscript generated by AI.
   - MDPI: Acknowledgments.
   - *Maedica*: caps "AI use" at 30%, alongside a plagiarism limit under 15%.
6. **Reporting-guideline mandates.** ERJ/ERJ OR require submitted checklists: CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA/MOOSE, TRIPOD (and STARD and SPIRIT where they apply). IJTLD requires CONSORT and STROBE. Respiratory Medicine and MDPI require a CONSORT checklist and flow diagram for RCTs. *Farmacia* requires PRISMA for reviews. No checklist mandate was found for RJME or *Maedica*.
7. **APCs** (Romania is high-income, so no Research4Life discount applies; see uncertainties):

   | Venue | Cost to publish |
   |---|---|
   | ERJ | Free under the subscription route; open access costs €2,665 (CC BY-NC) or €3,640 (CC BY) |
   | ERJ Open Research | Gold OA: €655 (CC BY-NC) or €1,495 (CC BY) for originals submitted from 1 Apr 2026; 10% discount (15% after 1 Apr 2025) for ERS-member corresponding authors |
   | Thorax | £4,500 for optional open access (snippet) |
   | AJRCCM | Publication fee even under the standard licence (snippet; amount not retrieved) |
   | IJTLD | Free within the page limit; €380 per excess page |
   | *Medicina* | CHF 2,200 |
   | *Farmacia* | 1,500 RON / €320 |
   | *Pneumologia* | Free |

8. **Delisting and predatory risk for Romanian authors is concrete:**
   - Romanian Journal of Oral Rehabilitation was **removed from WoS on 10 Jul 2025** for publishing general-medicine articles outside its scope.
   - *Revista de Chimie* was **discontinued in Scopus as of 2020** and is not in WoS Core in 2026.
   - MDPI titles: IJERPH was removed from WoS in 2023, and Journal of Personalized Medicine in Dec 2024 for "content relevance".
   - The lesson for the overlay: **check scope fit and current index status at submission time**, against the Clarivate MJL, the Scopus discontinued list, the NLM Catalog and UEFISCDI. Do not rely on a journal's self-reported indexing. (Farmacia's own indexing page lists SSCI, A&HCI and others for a pharmacy journal. UEFISCDI lists only SCIE.)

## Findings with citations

### International respiratory journals

**ERJ** — <https://publications.ersnet.org/authors/erj/instructions> (live)
- Article-type table: original research 3,000 words, 8 figures and tables, 40 references, abstract "Yes, 250 words".
- RCTs must be prospectively registered (ICMJE). "Epidemiological studies should be conducted and reported according to STROBE … A completed STROBE checklist should be included." PRISMA/MOOSE apply to SR/MA, and TRIPOD to prediction models, each with a checklist.
- Open access: "standard open access service costs EUR 2665 … CC BY-NC"; "Mandated option … CC BY … EUR 3640". Without OA, articles become free after an 18-month embargo.

**ERS shared preparation guide** — <https://publications.ersnet.org/authors/manuscript-preparation> (live)
- Abstract "of 250 words or fewer … four separate paragraphs, which correspond to: the question addressed …; materials/patients and methods; results; and the answer to the question."
- References "should conform to the style used in Index Medicus (Vancouver style)", numbered in square brackets.
- Optional plain-language summary of up to 300 words. Graphical abstracts must be submitted at peer review.
- RCTs must conform to CONSORT, with the checklist. Other designs (STARD, STROBE, SPIRIT, …) "should consult".

**ERS generative-AI policy** — <https://publications.ersnet.org/authors/generative-artificial-intelligence> (live; "This version: March 2026"). Quick-reference table:

| Use | Declared at submission | Declared in manuscript |
|---|---|---|
| Grammar/spellcheck | Yes | No |
| Translation | Yes | No |
| Drafting/structuring text | Yes | Yes, Acknowledgements |
| Data analysis/code | Yes | Yes, Methods |
| Flowcharts/diagrams | Yes | Yes, figure legend |
| Experimental photos | Not allowed | — |

Also: "Authors must not upload any material containing personally identifiable information (PII), patient data … to any AI service unless they are using a dedicated, private 'enterprise' instance."

**ERJ Open Research** — <https://publications.ersnet.org/authors/erjor/instructions> (live)
- Same 3,000-word, 250-word-abstract limits as ERJ.
- APC table for submissions from 1 Apr 2026, original articles and reviews:

  | Licence | Standard | ERS member | Research4Life Group B | Research4Life Group A |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | CC BY-NC | €655 | €557 | €328 | Free |
  | CC BY | €1,495 | — | — | — |

- "European Respiratory Society members paying the standard price will receive a 10% discount (15% for articles submitted after 1 April 2025."
- DOAJ independently states "up to 1495 EUR" (<https://doaj.org/toc/2312-0541>, snippet).

**ERS read-and-publish** — <https://publications.ersnet.org/institutions/open-access-agreements> (live). Deals exist through Jisc and through EIFL for 37 countries. The EIFL eligible-country page (<https://eifl.net/apcs/european-respiratory-journals-free-open-access-publishing>, live) does **not** list Romania: a grep for "Romania" found nothing.

**AJRCCM** — <https://academic.oup.com/ajrccm/pages/author-guidelines> (snippet; the live page is behind Cloudflare)
- "Structured abstract—maximum word count: 250 … Preferred/suggested abstract headers: Rationale, Objectives, Methods, Measurements and Main Results, Conclusions."
- "the Journal charges a publication fee to publish under a standard license."
- The ATS–OUP partnership was announced in 2025 (<https://site.thoracic.org/press-releases/the-american-thoracic-society-partners-with-oxford-university-press>, snippet). The old `atsjournals.org/page/ajrccm/instructions_for_authors` URL is therefore stale.
- AI policy, from the ATS editorial *Am J Respir Crit Care Med* 211(1):1 (<https://academic.oup.com/ajrccm/article/211/1/1/8513343>, snippet): authors "are currently required to transparently document their use of AI tools, in any form, and must describe any use of LLM tools in the Methods, A[cknowledgments]…"

**CHEST** — <https://journal.chestnet.org/authinfo_prep> (snapshot 2025-06-29)
- Original research abstract sections: "Background / Research Question / Study Design and Methods / Results / Interpretation / Clinical Trial Registration".
- "CHEST follows the AMA Manual of Style (11th ed)". References are superscript numerals.
- "CHEST journal does not require any specific formatting at original submission." Review is double-anonymized.
- The text limit of 3,200 words comes from the official CHEST Original Research Formatting Guide PDF (snippet).
- The 300-word abstract limit and the mandatory GenAI declaration and Take-Home Points are reported only by a third party (<https://livewrite.app/journals/chest>, 3rd-party).
- The Elsevier GenAI declaration is confirmed on the sister journal CHEST Pulmonary's guide (<https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/chest-pulmonary/publish/guide-for-authors>, snippet).

**Thorax** — <https://thorax.bmj.com/pages/authors> (snippet; live page Cloudflare-blocked; the snapshot from 2026-05-19 did not render the article-type text)
- "Original research … Word count: up to 3,500 … Title: maximum of 20 words; Structured abstract: up to 250 … References: up to 40; Please include the key messages."
- "authors can choose to have their article published open access under a Creative Commons licence for 4,500 GBP."
- BMJ AI policy, <https://authors.bmj.com/policies/ai-use> (live): "Include an acknowledgement of AI use in the 'contributor' section … If the AI use was in the course of research, a fuller description should be included in the methods section." Inadequate declaration can lead to rejection or post-publication changes.

**Respiratory Medicine** — <https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/respiratory-medicine/publish/guide-for-authors> (snapshot 2024-07-13; live page Cloudflare-blocked)
- Structured abstract with no word limit stated.
- "Highlights should consist of 3 to 5 bullet points, each a maximum of 85 characters."
- 1–7 keywords.
- "References can be in any style or format as long as the style is consistent" at submission. The house style is numbered in square brackets.
- "Authors must provide the CONSORT checklist at manuscript submission with an accompanying flow diagram."
- GenAI use must be declared in a section titled "Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process", placed before the references. "We do not permit the use of Generative AI or AI-assisted tools to create or alter images."

**IJTLD** — Instructions for Authors 2025 PDF, <https://theunion.org/sites/default/files/2025-06/Instructions-for-Authors%20IJTLD%202025%20Updated.pdf> (live)
- Original article: 2,500 words, 200-word abstract with headings "Background, Methods, Results and Conclusion". "Abstracts will be translated into French."
- Superscript numbered references: list all authors when there are three or fewer, otherwise the first author + "et al."
- "There are no fees for articles that meet our recommended page lengths"; "an excess page charge of 380€ per page … over six journal pages for Original articles" (<https://theunion.org/our-work/journals/ijtld/information-for-authors/journal-publication-charges>, live).
- Editorial policies (<https://theunion.org/our-work/journals/ijtld-open/editorial-policies>, live):
  - STROBE for epidemiological studies.
  - CONSORT, with WHO-registry trial registration, for clinical trials.
  - "We require authors to declare the proportion of their manuscript generated using AI."
  - Avoid stigmatising TB terms such as "TB suspect" and "defaulter".
- IJTLD Open and IJTLD CRD (chronic respiratory disease in low- and middle-income countries) are OA siblings (<https://theunion.org/our-work/journals/ijtld>, live).

### Romanian venues

***Pneumologia*** — <https://sciendo.com/journal/PNEUM> with the tabs `?tab=indexing`, `?tab=publication-information` and `?tab=for-authors` (live)
- Publisher: Romanian Society of Pneumology, in partnership with Paradigm Publishing Services. Language: English. eISSN 2247-059X.
- "There is no publication fee, and the online version is available open access."
- Volume-open model since vol. 72 (2023). At least two reviewers. Typically published within 3 months.
- The indexing tab lists Scopus, EBSCO, ProQuest, Dimensions, OpenAlex and other aggregators, but **not** WoS, PubMed or MEDLINE.
- NLM Catalog (ID 100941067): MEDLINE "Deselected", coverage "v48n4, Oct.-Dec. 1999 – v65n4, Oct.-Dec. 2016".
- Absent from both UEFISCDI WoS lists (2026).
- The "for authors" tab returned only aims and scope. Formatting instructions were **not retrievable**.

**RJME** — <https://rjme.ro/guide-for-authors/> and <https://rjme.ro/abstracting-and-indexing/> (live)
- "A concise abstract of no more 250 words … purpose of the research, the main results and major conclusions"; 3–6 keywords.
- References "indicated by numbers in square brackets … List names of all the authors … DOI, PMID and PMCID numbers are mandatory".
- Double-blind review. Scope is morphology and pathology, which fits respiratory work only when it is pathology-driven.
- UEFISCDI SCIE list: JIF 2025 1.3, Q4. NLM: MEDLINE currently indexed.

***Maedica*** — Instructions for authors PDF, <https://www.maedica.ro/wp-content/uploads/misc/Instructions_for_authors.pdf> (live)
- "written entirely in English"; abstract describing "Objectives, Materials and methods, Results and Conclusions".
- Manuscript "must not exceed 15 pages"; submitted by email to editor@maedica.ro.
- "the acceptable percentage of plagiarism report is below 15%, and the maximum accepted percentage for artificial intelligence (AI) use is 30%."
- References: house format.
- NLM: PubMed currently indexed, not MEDLINE. Absent from the UEFISCDI WoS lists.

***Farmacia*** — <https://farmaciajournal.com/information-for-authors/>, `/formating/`, `/article-charges/`, `/publication-ethics/` (live)
- "Title … must be drawn up both in Romanian and English (if none of the authors is Romanian, the editorial team can provide a translation for the title and abstract)."
- "Abstract (not exceeding 1500 characters) and 3 – 4 keywords; both must be written in Romanian and English."
- "All references should comply with Vancouver Style."
- PRISMA for reviews; ICMJE trial registration.
- "all published articles will be charged with a fee of 1500 RON (320 EURO)".
- UEFISCDI SCIE: JIF 2025 1.6, Q4.

***Medicina* (MDPI)** — <https://www.mdpi.com/journal/medicina/instructions> and `/apc` (live)
- "The abstract should be a total of about 300 words maximum … single paragraph … structured abstracts, with headings: Background and Objectives; Materials and Methods; …"
- "Your references may be in any style" at submission; ACS-style list.
- "MDPI requires a completed CONSORT 2025 checklist and flow diagram" for RCTs.
- "APC of CHF 2200".
- MDPI AI policy: "fully transparent, within the 'Acknowledgments' section" (<https://www.mdpi.com/news/5687>, snippet).

***Revista de Chimie***
- SCImago: "Discontinued in Scopus as of 2020" (<https://www.scimagojr.com/journalsearch.php?q=21496&tip=sid>, snippet).
- Not in the UEFISCDI SCIE or ESCI lists (2026). The NLM Catalog has no indexing source.
- revistadechimie.ro was unreachable on 2026-10-10.

### Congresses

**ERS** — FAQ <https://www.ersnet.org/frequently-asked-questions-about-abstracts/> and submission page <https://www.ersnet.org/congress-and-events/congress/abstract-and-clinical-case-submission/> (live)
- "The maximum length of an abstract is 1810 characters, including characters and spaces in the following: abstract body, table, and figures."
- "The character count of the title is separate … limited to 250 characters."
- "If an image/picture/figure is inserted, it deducts 277 characters."
- "A maximum of three abstracts … by the same person."
- Late-breaking abstracts cost €75 and RCT abstracts €150.
- "All accepted abstracts will be published in a supplement of the ERJ."

**SRP** — <https://www.congres-srp.ro/rezumate/> (live, published 2026-07-10)
- "rezumatele vor fi redactate atât în limba română, cât și în limba engleză (un singur document pentru ambele)" (abstracts are written in both Romanian and English, in one document for both).
- "corpul rezumatului nu trebuie să depășească 300 de cuvinte pentru fiecare limbă" (the abstract body must not exceed 300 words per language).
- "nu se vor folosi tabele sau grafică" (no tables or graphics).
- "Rezumatele selectate pot fi dezvoltate … articol … în revista Pneumologia" (selected abstracts can be developed into an article for *Pneumologia*).
- Deadline 30 Sep 2026. The congress runs 11–14 Nov 2026 (<https://www.congres-srp.ro/>, live).

### Delisting and predatory risk

- **Romanian Journal of Oral Rehabilitation** — official statement of 31 Jul 2025, <https://rjor.ro/official-statement-31-07-2025/> (live). Clarivate began a re-evaluation on 5 Jun 2025 "following a complaint concerning the presence of articles related to general medicine". "As of July 10, 2025 … the journal is no longer indexed in Web of Science." An appeal was pending, and authors with articles in the pipeline were offered refunds. This is a Romanian precedent: *medical papers placed in a scope-mismatched journal*.
- **UBB Scientific Council factual report** on Gold OA publishers and ranking withdrawals (Jun 2026), <https://cercetare.ubbcluj.ro/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/raport_OA_si_rankinguri_CS_site.pdf> (live, Romanian). It reports:
  - about 34% of Romanian-affiliated articles were in MDPI in 2021 (citing *Scientometrics* 2022);
  - about 20,000 Romanian ISI articles in 2025, about 40% of them with a single Gold OA publisher (MDPI);
  - IJERPH was delisted from WoS in 2023;
  - Clarivate notified MDPI on 10 Dec 2024 that J Pers Med was delisted from vol. 14(8) for "Content Relevance", with a 2-year embargo;
  - Hindawi/Wiley closed 19 journals;
  - Romanian evaluation bodies (CNFIS, CNATDCU) apply "almost exclusively numerical" criteria.
- **UEFISCDI scientometrics page** — <https://uefiscdi.gov.ro/scientometrie-reviste> (live). Publishes JIF/AIS quartile lists that Romanian evaluation uses. In 2026, 51 Romanian journals are in SCIE/SSCI/AHCI and 87 in ESCI.

## Implications for the overlay

1. **Venue registry entries** (review-criteria and format profiles) should cover at least ERJ, ERJ OR, AJRCCM, CHEST, Thorax, Respiratory Medicine, IJTLD, *Pneumologia*, *Medicina*, *Farmacia* and RJME, plus two congress profiles (ERS and SRP). Each entry needs these fields:
   - `abstract.structure`: headings list, or `unheaded_paragraphs: 4` for ERS journals;
   - `abstract.limit` with `unit: words|characters`;
   - `abstract.languages`: e.g. `[ro, en]` for *Farmacia* and SRP;
   - `text_limit`, `ref_limit`, `extras`: highlights (Elsevier), key messages (Thorax), plain-language summary and graphical abstract (ERS);
   - `citation_style`: Vancouver, AMA, IJTLD-variant, RJME house format with mandatory DOI/PMID/PMCID, *Maedica* house format, ACS (MDPI);
   - `reporting_checklists_required`;
   - `ai_disclosure`: location map and any thresholds;
   - `apc`;
   - `indexing`: each value with a verified-on date and source.
2. **Character-count limits must be first-class.** ERS (1810 characters, with table and figure penalties) and *Farmacia* (1500 characters per language) cannot be handled by word-count checks. The SRP limit is *per language*, so one bilingual document holds two 300-word bodies.
3. **The `ro-en` pair is load-bearing for only two targets**: *Farmacia* and the SRP congress. Both require the Romanian and English versions to *match*, so the pack needs a parallel-abstract consistency check rather than free translation. For every international journal, Romanian output is author-facing only (drafting and explanation), never submission text.
4. **AI disclosure is a per-venue output, not a constant.** ARS's own use (drafting, translation, structuring) triggers a declaration at ERS journals *even for translation*. The overlay should emit the declaration text and its placement for each venue: Acknowledgements/Methods/figure legend (ERS); a dedicated pre-reference section (Elsevier); contributor section (BMJ); Methods/Acknowledgments (ATS/OUP, MDPI); a proportion statement (IJTLD). *Maedica*'s "≤30% AI" cap has no defined metric, so the overlay should flag it for author review and not claim compliance.
5. **Data-protection gate.** ERS bans uploading patient data or PII into non-enterprise AI tools. This fits the planned ethics/GDPR layer and should be a hard pre-flight question before ARS ingests clinical datasets.
6. **Reporting-guideline gate.** Map study design to its EQUATOR checklist (STROBE, CONSORT, PRISMA/MOOSE, TRIPOD, STARD, CARE for case reports) and mark it *required with submission* for ERS journals, IJTLD, Respiratory Medicine (RCTs) and MDPI (RCTs).
7. **Venue-status gate (anti-delisting).** Before recommending a Romanian or OA venue, check and date-stamp:
   - (a) Clarivate MJL;
   - (b) the Scopus discontinued list;
   - (c) NLM Catalog indexing status;
   - (d) the current UEFISCDI SCIE/ESCI list;
   - (e) **scope fit** between manuscript and journal (the RJOR precedent).

   Never trust a journal's self-reported indexing (*Farmacia* and *Pneumologia* both show gaps between self-description and the registries). This stays thin and venue-only, per the map's "academic-career pressure" layer.
8. ***Pneumologia* positioning.** Frame it as the free, English, SRP-linked venue and the natural home for SRP-congress follow-ups and resident prize papers. Say plainly that it is Scopus-only, not in WoS Core, and no longer in MEDLINE. The overlay cannot ship a complete *Pneumologia* profile until open uncertainty 1 is resolved.
