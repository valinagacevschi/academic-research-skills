# Venue landscape for Romanian respiratory publishing

Type: research
Status: resolved
Map: ../map.md

## Question

Which venues do Romanian respiratory researchers target, and what does each require? Cover: international respiratory journals (ERJ, ERJ Open Research, Chest, AJRCCM, Thorax, Respiratory Medicine, IJTLD, etc.), *Pneumologia* (Sciendo), and Romanian medical journals (e.g. Romanian Journal of Morphology and Embryology, Maedica, Revista de Chimie, Farmacia, Medicina-like venues popular in RO). For each: indexing (WoS/JCR, Scopus, PubMed), language, abstract format and word limits, whether a Romanian abstract is required, reporting-guideline mandates, AI-use disclosure policy, citation style, and APCs. Also the abstract formats for the ERS Congress and the Romanian Society of Pneumology (SRP) congress. Note any predatory or delisted-journal risks relevant to RO authors.

## Answer

- **Three tiers:** international respiratory journals (ERJ, ERJ OR, AJRCCM, CHEST, Thorax, Resp Med, IJTLD); MDPI open-access titles such as *Medicina*, which took about 40% of Romania's ~20k WoS articles in 2025; and Romanian journals (*Pneumologia*, RJME, *Maedica*, *Farmacia*, RJIM).
- ***Pneumologia*:** English-only open access, in Scopus only (no WoS, out of MEDLINE since 2016); the SRP congress feeds abstracts into it.
- **Romanian abstract:** required only by *Farmacia* and the SRP congress (RO plus EN, 300 words each).
- **Abstract formats** vary widely; the ERS Congress counts 1810 characters including spaces, not words.
- **AI disclosure** is universal, with varying detail. ERS bans putting patient data into non-enterprise AI tools; IJTLD asks for the share of AI-written text; *Maedica* caps AI use at 30%.
- **Delisting and indexing risk is real** (a Romanian journal removed from WoS in 2025, *Rev Chim* out of Scopus, MDPI journals delisted, self-claimed indexing wrong), so check index status and scope fit at submission time.
- **Caveat:** four major journals blocked fetches, and *Pneumologia*'s author instructions couldn't be retrieved.
